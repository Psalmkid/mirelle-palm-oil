About Mirelle

We keep our focus on the good stuff.

Mirelle Palm oil Limited exists to make one thing well: supply palm oil that households and businesses can trust, in sizes that fit the way they cook.

From Lagos, for real kitchens.

We believe quality should feel straightforward. That means carefully selected oil, clean handling, secure packaging and a buying experience that does not get in the way.

What guides us

  • Consistency: a familiar colour and flavour from bottle to bottle.
  • Useful choice: compact sizes for homes and larger formats for trade.
  • Care: clear communication from basket to delivery.

Built with intention

Mirelle is growing one dependable order at a time, with people and kitchens at the centre of the work.
