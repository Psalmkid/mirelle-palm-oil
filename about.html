About Mirelle

We keep our focus on the good stuff.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, we are committed to supplying quality palm oil that households, food businesses, restaurants, retailers, and international buyers can rely on. Our goal is simple: to make dependable palm oil available in convenient sizes and quantities that fit the needs of customers at home and around the world.

From Lagos, for real kitchens.

From careful selection to clean handling and secure packaging, we pay attention to the details that matter. Every product is prepared with quality, consistency, and customer satisfaction in mind. Whether you are purchasing for your home, growing a food business, supplying customers locally, or sourcing palm oil for international markets, Mirelle is built to make quality palm oil easier to access.

What guides us

  • Consistency: the familiar colour, flavour, and quality our customers expect with every purchase.
  • Choice: convenient sizes for households and larger quantities for businesses, wholesale buyers, and export needs.
  • Care: clear communication, secure packaging, careful handling, and attention to every order from purchase to delivery.
  • Global reach: rooted in Lagos, with a vision to connect quality Nigerian palm oil with customers and businesses around the world.

Built with intention

Mirelle is being built one dependable order at a time. We are creating a palm-oil brand that people can trust — not only for the quality of our products, but also for the reliability and care behind every order. As we grow, our vision is to build strong relationships with customers, retailers, businesses, distributors, and international buyers while bringing quality palm oil from Nigeria to a wider market.

Meet the Founder

Faleye Mistura

Mirelle Palm Oil Limited was founded with a clear vision: to provide quality palm oil that households and businesses can confidently rely on, both locally and internationally. Faleye Mistura, Founder of Mirelle Palm Oil Limited, is building the company around the values of quality, reliability, customer satisfaction, and sustainable growth.

From its foundation in Lagos, Mirelle is growing with a broader vision — to serve customers within Nigeria while developing opportunities to reach international markets.

“We are building Mirelle one dependable order at a time, with quality and our customers at the heart of everything we do.”
— Faleye Mistura, Founder, Mirelle Palm Oil Limited
